2. Product Overview
The AI-Based Meeting Minutes Generator is a web-based application that automatically converts meeting transcripts into structured meeting minutes using Generative AI. The system accepts a meeting transcript as input and uses an LLM to generate a meeting summary, key discussion points, important decisions, action items, responsible persons/owners, and deadlines. The system also uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to allow users to search previous meeting records semantically.
3. Problem Statement
Preparing meeting minutes manually is time-consuming and can lead to missing important decisions, action items, and deadlines. Meeting records can also become difficult to search as their number increases. The proposed product addresses these problems by automatically generating structured meeting minutes and providing semantic search across historical meeting records.
4. Product Goals
Automatically summarize meeting transcripts.

15. Ethical Considerations
Meeting transcripts may contain sensitive or confidential information. The system should handle stored data carefully and restrict access. AI-generated minutes should be reviewed because LLMs may sometimes hallucinate, misrepresent statements, or omit important context. Generated minutes should therefore not automatically be treated as the official record without human verification.

17. Conclusion
The AI-Based Meeting Minutes Generator aims to reduce the time and effort required to prepare meeting minutes. By combining Generative AI, LLM-based summarization, embeddings, vector databases, and RAG, the system will transform unstructured meeting transcripts into useful, searchable, and structured records for students, faculty, and small teams.
